# Responsive Grounds

This mod adds particle effects to movements and actions depending on the ground. Leaves swirl around your feet in Riften’s autumn forests, inspired by Ghost of Tsushima, while dirt, dust and small rocks fly up as you move across different types of terrain.

Features
-  Walking, running, sprinting and riding kick up different amounts of particles. Leaves flutter down, rocks bounce, and dust lingers in the air
- Horses, NPCs and creatures also interact with the ground. Giants and mammoths create bigger effects
- Weapon swings stir up leaves from the ground
- Shouts push particles away from the player
- Winds can lift leaves and blow them (Highly recommended Dynamic Wind Framework - SKSE Plugin )
- Northern Roads support.
- Adjust particle amounts, sizes and limits. You can also add or remove landscape textures for each particle type.
