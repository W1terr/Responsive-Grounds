This mod adds particle effects to movements and actions based on the ground beneath you. Leaves swirl around your feet in Riften’s autumn forests, inspired by *Ghost of Tsushima*, while dirt, dust, and small rocks fly up as you move across different types of terrain.

## Features
=======
SKSE plugin for Skyrim SE / AE: the ground answers to everything that moves on it. Fallen leaves swirl up behind you in Riften's autumn forest (inspired by Ghost of Tsushima), green leaves in the pine forests, snow flakes on snow, dirt and dust fly from dirt roads and mud, pebbles and rock dust from rocky ground.

- **Steps**: things fly up with each footfall, a few when you walk, many more when you run, sprint or ride. They ease in after half a second of moving, swirl in the air you drag along, flutter or drop back down and fade away. Ground you just went over has fewer leaves left for a while.
- **Leaves** on Riften's autumn forest textures; **green leaves** on the pine forest textures (add them to any texture you like); **dust** (clods that bounce once, dust puffs) on dirt and mud; **rocks** (pebbles, rock dust) on rocky ground; **snow** flakes on snow (they melt soon after landing).
- **Objects** count too: cliffs, mountains, rocks and ground pieces by the landscape textures painted on them (or their own dirt, rock and snow textures, or their surface material). Up on things that aren't ground (log piles, buildings, docks) nothing is kicked up.
- **Water**: leaves float on it and drift.
- **Your horse, NPCs and creatures** kick things up as well (the nearest ones; giants and mammoths over a wider area).
- **Weapon swings** stir up leaves and a little dust under the blade (works with vanilla, MCO, SCAR and Precision attacks).
- **Shouts** blow everything away in front of the shouter; **heavy blows** on the ground (giant clubs, hammers), **explosions** (fireballs, runes, traps) and **falling bodies** throw up a ring.
- **Sounds**: a soft rustle of flying leaves and little stones ticking as pebbles land (dust and snow stay quiet).
- **Wind** (optional): strong wind lifts leaves and carries them across your view; works with Dynamic Wind.
- **Northern Roads** support: its roads kick up dust and pebbles by kind of road, on their own menu page.
- Every landscape texture can be added to or taken out of each kind, with its own amount, in the menu.
- Menu in English, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Polish, Russian, Japanese and Chinese.

- Walking, running, sprinting, and riding kick up different amounts of particles. Leaves flutter down, rocks bounce, and dust lingers in the air.
- Horses, NPCs, and creatures also interact with the ground. Giants and mammoths create larger effects.
- Weapon swings stir up leaves from the ground.
- Shouts push particles away from the player.
- Strong winds can lift leaves and carry them through the air. **Dynamic Wind Framework - SKSE Plugin is highly recommended.**
- Support for Northern Roads.
- Customize particle amounts, sizes, and limits. You can also add or remove landscape textures for each particle type.
